# Forms

The IRS forms the guides name (requirements S12.7.8). Each heading is a form and its title; the
line under it, only a link, is the form's page on irs.gov. A guide's mention of a form links
there: "Form 8949" and "Schedule D" in full, and "1099-DIV" or "W-2" alone, since they can be
nothing else; a bare number ("8949") is never taken for a form. Kept in alphabetical order; a test
fails when a guide names a form missing here.
