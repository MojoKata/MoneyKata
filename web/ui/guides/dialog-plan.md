# Edit plan

The plan from the month shown on. Earlier months keep the plan they had.

## Category

A category plans its children too: plan _Food_ for groceries and dining together, or each on its
own.

## Amount

What you mean to spend in a month, or a year for a yearly line. Income categories plan what you
expect to receive.

## Every

**Month** compares with each month; **Year** with the year so far, for costs that come once or twice
a year.

## Rollover

Carry what is left at the end of a month into the next, or what was overspent out of it.
