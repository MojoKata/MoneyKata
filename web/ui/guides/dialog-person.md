# A person

Someone you give gifts to. They needn't be a user of this server.

## Name

What they're called in your lists.

## Relationship

Daughter, father, friend: how they are related to you.

## Linked user {#linked-user}

When they are a user of this server: gifts for them are never shown to them, here or anywhere else,
even through a share.

## Birthday

Their birthday, with the year if you know it (for their age), or without it: 10-20.

## Yearly gift budget {#gift-budget}

What you mean to spend on them in a year, all occasions together.

## Holidays

The holidays they get gifts for. Dates are worked out each year: fixed ones, rule-based ones like
Mother's Day, and lunar or lunisolar ones like Diwali from shipped tables.

## Anniversaries and other dates {#dates}

A wedding anniversary, a name day, any other date of their own, each with a budget of its own if you
like.

## Remind me {#lead-days}

How long before a date it shows on the Dashboard and raises an alert, until something is bought.

## Notes

Sizes, likes, what they already have.

## Given before

What you gave them in past years, so it isn't given twice.
