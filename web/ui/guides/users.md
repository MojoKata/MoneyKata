# Users and Groups

For admins: who uses this server and the groups shares are given to.

![Users and Groups](shots/users.webp)

## Asked to be made active again

People whose account is inactive and who asked, from the sign-in screen, to be made active.

## Show

**Active** users, or **all** of them with the inactive ones.

## New user

A name, username and email: they get an invitation and choose their own password. Admins never set passwords.

## Resend invitation

A new invitation link, for someone who hasn't joined yet.

## Edit

A user's name and groups, and their email before they join.

## Active {#active}

Off ends the user's sessions at once and stops them signing in; shares they hold give them nothing while they're inactive. Users are made inactive, never deleted. The last active admin can't be.

## New group

A named set of users that accounts and portfolios can be shared with. **Admins** is built in: its members manage users, groups and Settings, and see others' money only when it's shared with them.
