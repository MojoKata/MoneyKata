# New projection

It starts as a scenario, with figures to change; make it active to follow it.

## Goal type {#goal}

**Retirement**, for you or a couple: the chance the money lasts. **Savings target**: the chance of
an amount by a date.

## Name

Such as _Retire at 63_ or _House deposit_.

## Built on

A portfolio, an account, or a typed amount for a what-if.

## Amount {#typed-amount}

The typed amount.

## Who

You, or you and your spouse (set in Profile for wash sales): each with a birth year and retirement
age.
