# New tax book

One tax year's record, for you, or for you and your spouse filing jointly.

## Year

The tax year.

## Filing jointly

With your spouse (from your relationships in Profile), married on the last day of the year. They're
asked to accept before the book counts their accounts.

## Start from last year

Copies last year's accounts left out or added, and makes its received documents this year's expected
ones.
