# New account

Choose the account's type. It decides the fields, the tax treatment and the tabs.

## Banking

Checking, savings, money market and CDs: cash, taxable interest.

## Credit card

A liability: a balance you owe shows as negative.

## Brokerage

Stocks, ETFs and funds, taxable.

## Retirement

Traditional and Roth IRAs, 401(k), 403(b) and 457(b) plans, and HSAs: contributions record their tax year.
