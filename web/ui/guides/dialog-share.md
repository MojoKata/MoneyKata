# Share

Who else has this item, and at which level. Nothing changes until **Save**.

## People

Every active user. **Read** sees everything and changes nothing; **Write** also changes what it holds. Nobody but you deletes it, shares it or sees its keys.

## Via a group

Someone with access through a group is shown with the group's name.

## Groups

The same, for every member while they're in the group.

## Share these accounts too

A portfolio's viewers see only the accounts in it that are shared with them. This shares the missing ones you own, at Read.
